Frlands Fornåmwoorden
Farlandic Pronouns

Personal Pronouns

Farlandic English
Ick I
Djů / Jy you
Hy / Sy / at he / she / it
wy we
jy y'all
sy they

Subject Pronouns

Subject pronouns come before the verb and show who does the action.

Ick bem op Heem.

I am home.

Djů gås op Sjol.

You go to school.

Jy hebben Bir end sy hebben Wåter.

Y'all have and they have water.

Object Pronouns

Object pronouns receive the action of the verb.

Farlandic English
my me
dy you
hem / har / at him / her / it
ons us
jů you
hen them

Ick se dy.

I see you.

Reflexive Pronouns

Farlandic English

dansyf yourself

I see myself.

Question Pronouns

Farlandic English

wårm why
